Reproducing the paper

The library was developed together with the paper P. Torrente et al., Factorized approximate inverse preconditioners in the tensor train format (in preparation), which introduces the factorized approximate inverse (FAI) preconditioner of fai_preconditioner and tests it on the anisotropic elliptic operators of its Sec. 5.1 and on the Chorin projection around a cylinder of its Sec. 5.2. The scripts that generate the figures and tables of the paper are in the repository, in experiments/paper, and its README.md lists, for each figure and table, the script that produces it, the environment, and the SLURM settings of the runs on the cluster.

The figures of the current version of the paper were produced with the library as of the tag paper-ttfai-data (commit 6850540) and the scripts of the paper repository. The library has changed since then. Several of the changes alter numbers, for instance the orientation of the FAI factor and the convention of the cutoff of ResidualALS, and the arguments of some functions changed. The scripts of experiments/paper follow the current API, so their results need not agree digit by digit with the data of that tag, and the two sets of results must not be mixed in one collection or plot. The changes are listed in the release notes (NEWS.md), and the README of experiments/paper lists the three that matter for the paper.

The data of the paper are regenerated with the release version of the library before submission. This is still open: the commit that produces the new data, and the place where they are archived (with its DOI), are not yet fixed, and we will state them here and in the README of experiments/paper once the runs are done.

The heavy runs of the paper need a cluster and are not part of the documentation build or of the tests. The experiment scripts also have a smoke mode, with tiny sizes, which only checks that they run.
